# HR Analytics Dashboard Explanation

## Dashboard Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of employee demographics, workforce distribution, salary information, hiring trends, and leave management. The visualizations help HR teams understand employee composition and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 1. Head Count by Job Title

### Visualization Type

Bar Chart

### Description

This chart displays the total number of employees across different job titles within the organization.

### Key Insights

* Packaging Associates represent the largest employee group.
* Production Operators and Sales Representatives also contribute significantly to the workforce.
* Marketing Managers have comparatively fewer employees.
* Helps identify workforce distribution across job roles.

### Business Value

Supports workforce planning, recruitment strategies, and department-level resource allocation.

---

## 2. Employee Salary Details

### Visualization Type

Table

### Description

The table displays employee-level details including Employee ID, Employee Name, Job Title, and Salary.

### Key Insights

* Provides a detailed salary overview for individual employees.
* Enables comparison of compensation across similar job roles.
* Helps identify salary variations within departments.

### Business Value

Useful for compensation analysis, payroll review, and HR audits.

---

## 3. Head Count by Gender

### Visualization Type

Pie Chart

### Description

This chart illustrates the gender distribution of employees within the organization.

### Key Insights

* Female employees account for approximately 54.66% of the workforce.
* Male employees account for approximately 45.34%.
* Indicates a relatively balanced gender distribution.

### Business Value

Supports diversity and inclusion analysis and helps track gender representation.

---

## 4. Head Count by Age Group and Gender

### Visualization Type

Clustered Column Chart

### Description

The chart categorizes employees into age groups and compares gender distribution within each age category.

### Key Insights

* Most employees fall within the 25–35 age range.
* Employee representation decreases in higher age groups.
* Male and female employee counts are relatively balanced in the major age categories.

### Business Value

Assists HR teams in workforce planning, succession planning, and demographic analysis.

---

## 5. Head Count by Year

### Visualization Type

Line Chart

### Description

This chart shows employee hiring trends based on the year employees joined the organization.

### Key Insights

* Hiring increased steadily from 2017 to 2019.
* Workforce growth remained strong through 2020.
* Hiring activity declined gradually after the peak period.
* Reflects changes in recruitment trends over time.

### Business Value

Helps analyze hiring effectiveness and supports future recruitment planning.

---

## 6. Average Leave Balance by Job Title

### Visualization Type

Horizontal Bar Chart

### Description

This chart displays the average leave balance for employees across different job titles.

### Key Insights

* Packaging Associates have the highest average leave balance.
* Sales Representatives and Production Operators also maintain high leave balances.
* Certain roles show lower leave balances, indicating higher leave utilization.

### Business Value

Supports leave management analysis and helps HR identify departments that may require workload balancing or leave policy review.

---

## Overall Dashboard Insights

* The workforce is distributed across multiple job roles, with Packaging Associates forming the largest group.
* Gender representation is relatively balanced across the organization.
* Most employees belong to the 25–35 age category, indicating a young workforce.
* Hiring activity peaked around 2019–2020 and declined in subsequent years.
* Leave balance patterns vary across job roles, highlighting differences in leave utilization.
* Salary data provides transparency into employee compensation and supports HR decision-making.

---

## Conclusion

The HR Analytics Dashboard transforms employee data into meaningful insights by combining workforce demographics, hiring trends, salary information, and leave management metrics. The dashboard enables HR professionals and management teams to monitor workforce health, improve planning, and make informed strategic decisions.
